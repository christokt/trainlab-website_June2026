Dr. Christo K Thomas

Assistant Professor, Worcester Polytechnic Institute

Dr. Thomas completed his postdoctoral research at Virginia Tech and earned his Ph.D. from EURECOM, France. He holds an M.S. from the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, and a B.S. from NIT Calicut. His research interests include semantic communications, statistical signal processing, and machine learning for wireless communications. Before academia, he contributed to 4G LTE, 5G modem design, and advanced wireless R&D at Broadcom, Intel, and Qualcomm.

Albert Shaju

PhD Student in Electrical & Computer Engineering

Albert is a PhD student at WPI. He earned his B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from the National Institute of Technology (NIT), Calicut. Master's Students

Vaansh Mansharamani

Master's Student in Artificial Intelligence, Worcester Polytechnic Institute

Vaansh is a master’s student in Artificial Intelligence at WPI. He earned his bachelor’s degree in Computer Science at WPI, where he completed his MQP through a joint study with Kyoto University of Advanced Science on using neural networks to map wall-interaction forces onto drones. His current research applies artificial intelligence to semantic communication in multi-drone systems, supported by a simulation platform he helped develop for data collection and experimentation.
